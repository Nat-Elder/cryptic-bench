RESULT:     the letters your wordplay actually produces.
ANSWER:     your answer.

Rules. Spans tagged literal, and the DEFINITION and INDICATOR spans, must be copied from the clue exactly as written. Letters that come from an abbreviation or a synonym must be tagged abbrev or synonym, not literal. RESULT is what the wordplay gives, so if it does not match your ANSWER, say so rather than adjusting it.
